## Design Specificity Verdict

**The evidence layer is unmistakably this product. The vessel carrying it is a template.**

**LLM assessment.** What is genuinely specific: the case-study copy model in `components/work/WorkSection.tsx:12-62`. A `<dl>` of Status / Role / Focus, a named "Decision narrative" carrying a concrete scenario ("The meeting ran 45 minutes late…"), four proof chips naming real product decisions (Session Swapper, Wet Mode, Guest-first booking, **Live-build audit**), and a status line that volunteers its own weakness — *"Fictional solo concept · Working prototype, nothing shipped."* Nothing else could run this copy.

Everything above it could be lifted onto a motion studio, a sneaker drop, or an architecture practice without editing a pixel: a 195-frame pinned scroll hero, a Three.js ring field, orange/black brutalist uppercase, a full-bleed word marquee, scramble/decrypt text, dot-grid arrow buttons, a full-screen menu with a scanning-line preview. This is the 2023–25 awards-site shell, and it arrives *before* any of the specific material.

The system is internally coherent to a fault. Coherence has been achieved by making every chapter perform the same move: giant left-aligned two-line uppercase statement, one word in orange, small-caps index in a corner. Hero, About title, About lead, Expertise, Work, Contact — six times. By the third, the reader has learned the big type never carries information and starts skipping it. The system's consistency is training the visitor to ignore its most prominent element.

Structurally, five consecutive full-bleed scroll-jacked sequences — hero (195 frames), About portrait (Canvas 2D), About understory (80 frames), Work background (240 frames), marquee. 515 JPEGs across three separate frame players, and the visitor cannot tell them apart.

**The Aperture metaphor is asserted, not earned.** The HUD says "Scroll to focus", "00–100 depth", "195 frames" — but focus never resolves anything. The hero frames are a face turning; the About reveal pays back *the same photograph in grayscale*; the depth counter counts nothing the visitor cares about. The metaphor would be earned if scroll position governed **which claim was in focus and which was blurred** — demonstrating evidence triage instead of naming it.

## Overall Impression

This is a genuinely well-built site that is losing to itself. The engineering is real — a `prefers-reduced-motion` path that ships a *different, shorter, readable page* rather than a `transition: none` stub, a genuine focus trap, a frame-fallback chain, zero console errors. The positioning is real and rare: a designer who audits his own claims and publishes the gaps.

And a hiring manager scanning for 45 seconds on a phone gets none of it. They get 4.4 viewports of a face turning, a clipped headline, a domain line that is `display: none`, a CTA that goes nowhere, and — at the very end — the designer's own name rendered as **"ED BONILL"**.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] The design system's typeface never loads — and the fallback breaks layout in three places

`app/globals.css:7-8` defines `--font-syne: "Arial Black", …` and `--font-inter: "Helvetica Neue", …`. There is no `next/font` import anywhere and no font files in `public/assets/`. Every "Syne" surface renders in Arial Black, or whatever the OS substitutes — so **the page looks different on every machine it is opened on.**

Because Arial Black is far wider than Syne at `-0.065em`, this is not only an aesthetic loss. Measured at 375px (content box 20→355):

- `h1` line "Designing" — ink **345px**, right edge **365px** → breaks the gutter.
- Hero beat two "Clear experiences." — ink **447px** → **clipped 92px off-screen**, reads "CLEAR EXPERIENC".
- Closing signature "ED BONILLA" — ink **388px** in a **353px** `overflow: hidden` box → renders **"ED BONILL"**.

**Why it matters:** the recruiter's first impression (the `h1`) and last impression (the name) are both broken words, and the typographic identity actually being evaluated is simply absent.

**Fix:** add `next/font/google` for Syne (600, 700) and Inter (400–700) in `app/layout.tsx`, bind to `--font-syne` / `--font-inter`, re-verify the three measurements, and add `overflow-wrap: anywhere` plus a mobile `font-size` cap on `.hero-beat-statement` (`globals.css:1199-1203`) and `.signatureTrack`.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable typeset`

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`

### [P1] The primary CTA is a dead end, and two identical "LET'S TALK" buttons appear on one screen

### [P1] Accessibility defects that directly falsify the accessibility positioning

- **(a)** The single `h1`'s accessible name is `"Designingclarityinto motion."` — no spaces, because each line is a bare `<span style="display:block">` with no whitespace (`PortfolioHero.tsx:430-434`). - **(c)** The About section has **no heading element** — `#about-title` is a `<div>` (`AboutSection.tsx:831`). The heading outline never contains "About", "Edgar Bonilla", or "Selected work".
- **(d)** `.project-facts dt` is `#777e7e` on `#f2efe8` = **3.68:1** at 9px (detector: 3.6:1 ×6). Fails WCAG 1.4.3 — on the STATUS / ROLE / FOCUS labels, the honest-labelling apparatus itself.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit`

### [P2] About hides its only substance behind motion, and teaches a gesture that returns nothing

`AboutSection.tsx:891-930` puts **both bio paragraphs** on the back faces of cards that start at `rotateY(0)` and only flip on scroll, then reveal via `DecryptedText` character-by-character, across a `310svh` section (`globals.css:2300-2305`). `AboutSection.tsx:840-844` instructs "Hover or press / Reveal the layer below" — and the reveal returns the same photograph, desaturated. On mobile the accent line "SYSTEM CLARITY." in `#ff4f18` sits directly on orange-lit skin because `.about-image-shade` at ≤900px doesn't darken enough.

**Why it matters:** the paragraphs establishing seniority, domain, bilingual practice, and accessibility stance are the highest-value text on the page and the hardest to reach. A fast scroller passes them mid-flip and mid-decrypt and reads nothing.

**Fix:** render both paragraphs face-up as static type, keep the flip for the *label* only; cut `.about-understory` to ~150svh; either give the portrait reveal a real payload (annotate the portrait with the domain credentials it exists to prove) or delete it and reclaim the screen.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable quieter`
